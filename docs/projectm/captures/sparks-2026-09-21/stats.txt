Engine-only keyed frames at 3..8 s of the same demo voice (docs/projectm/shoot.sh). Metric: bright pixels = rgb*alpha
luminance > 70; "anisotropy" = sqrt(major/minor eigenvalue) of their covariance: ~1 = round cloud, >5 = a line.

diagnostic preset variants on native (q11 forced 4; sw1 init 4 instead of rand(8); fps literal 30): all still a vertical chain — not rand, not fps

Why: chain breaker offsets spark n by gmegabuf(2000+n) = a smoothed difference of spectrum bins n and n+dif. Neighbouring-bin
correlation of the two engines' spectra on the same 16 kHz samples (numpy replica of both FFT paths, docs/projectm/… fftratio.py):
  real speech: lag-1 r = 0.32 web vs 0.90 native; lag-2 0.27 vs 0.67.  demo voice: 0.56 vs 0.91.
projectM (like MilkDrop) FFTs 480 Hann-windowed, 2-tap-damped samples zero-padded to 1024 → adjacent bins agree → the 256 sparks
form a chain. butterchurn FFTs 1024 raw samples with no window → adjacent bins independent → the sparks scatter into a cloud.
Both engines apply the same custom-wave gain (0.15 × scaling × wave_scale) — checked in CustomWaveform.cpp and customWaveform.js.
projectm-eval's rand() is a Mersenne twister with a fixed seed (0x4141f00d), so the preset's sw1..sw4 are the same every run
(the chain always points the same way); forcing them did not change the shape, so it is not the cause of the chain.
