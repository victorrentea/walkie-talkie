# Mouse gestures and the two global keys

What `HotkeyTap` may and may not do with the two keyboard chords and with the mouse, in both gesture modes (*Use Logi Gestures* ticked — the default — and unticked). Full history and reasoning: docs/journal.md — see the sections named after each rule below.

## The two global keys: ⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates

| key | call | note |
|---|---|---|
| **⌘⌃B** | `onBindHotkey` → `bindFrontmostTerminal` | was ⌘⌃D until 2026-09-01 |
| **⌘⌃D** | `onLocalToggle` → `toggleLocalRecording` | **gone 2026-10-25 — the key passes through, starts and stops nothing** (Victor: *"ctr-cmd-d sa nu mai porneasca dictarea"*, then *no stop either*); supersedes every ⌘⌃D bullet below |

- **Swallow both unconditionally, autorepeat included.** A held ⌘⌃D would open the microphone and shut it again on the next repeat; a held ⌘⌃B would bind and then immediately stop the session it started. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **⌘⌃D is ungated at the tap.** A key that sometimes falls through to macOS's "look up in dictionary" would be worse than one that never does; with nothing bound a bound prompt does not start and the chip says `📍 Nothing bound — ⌘⌃B binds the terminal in front` (2026-11-22; it was held for a bind from 2026-09-11). → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **⌘⌃⌥D is Victor Mac' dark-mode toggle**, told apart by ⌥ alone. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **This app is the only owner of ⌘⌃B/⌘⌃D.** Two taps claiming one chord would both fire on one press; nothing about either key remains in Victor Mac (the cheat sheet lists it display-only). Both shadow the system-wide chord. → journal: *⌘⌃D is this app's own key (since 2026-08-26)*
- **⌘⌃B on the target already bound unbinds** (`bindFrontmostTerminal(toggle:)` defaults to the toggle); only the mouse chord passes `false`. → journal: *The wheel is the relay's; mouse 5 is nobody's (except in Replace Wispr)*
- **Both ride the menu as real key equivalents** (`StatusItem`): ⌘⌃B on **Connect Window**, ⌘⌃D on **Start Dictation** *and* **End Dictation** — only one of the two rows is ever enabled. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*

## ⌘⌃X — the local model, now (2026-09-28)

Victor: *"To compensate as a backup for slow transcriptions, I want a local fallback that I can
access during the dictation, at any point, through a key combination displayed in the tooltip … I
know it automatically falls back to local, but I'd like to have this faster whenever I want."*

| state | ⌘⌃X does |
|---|---|
| recording (ElevenLabs, ElevenLabs + Live, Wispr) | the microphone closes as the stop closes it (destination latched), **nothing is uploaded** — `ElevenLabsSource` marks the take (`forcedTakes`), Wispr is dismissed with ⌃Escape and its row held owned (`watchLateRow`); the relay's WAV goes to `fallBackToLocal` |
| settling (waiting on Scribe's upload or Wispr's row) | the wait is abandoned: the upload goes on and its answer is **only logged** (`💻 ElevenLabs answered N s after ⌘⌃X … only logged`), Wispr's row/⌘V land in the discard; the same WAV goes to the local model |
| idle, the local engine, or the local model already standing in | swallowed; one flash — `Nothing to transcribe locally` (`💻 Already on the local model`, `💻 Already transcribing on this Mac`) |

- **Why X and not L:** ⌘⌃L is Victor Mac' Google Calendar (`EventTapManager`), ⌘⌃W is Wispr
  Flow's *paste last transcript* (`config.json`: `[13, 55, 59]`), ⌘⌃C is Addons' Claude terminal and
  Wispr's copy. X is free in all three and reads as his own word for it, *quick exit*.
- **One path with the automatic fallback**: `source.handToLocal()` ends the take
  `.failed(why: DictationEnd.localForced, audio:)`; `fallBackToLocal` reads that `why` as *asked
  for* — no `⚠️ … was unavailable` warning on the words, `lastFailure` untouched, `via: local-forced`.
  The Q13 floor still holds: under 1.5 s voiced it is `heardNothing` → Recover.
- **Cold model:** the key still works — the press starts `bringUpModel()` and `transcribeLocally`
  waits up to 90 s for the weights (the fallback's own bank). The chip says `(loading)`.
- **Swallowed always, autorepeat included, dropped while main is frozen** (`isOwnChordWhileFrozen`).
  Handed on `DispatchQueue.main`, not through `ToggleTicket` — it is not a toggle. Menu row
  **Transcribe Locally Now** (⌘⌃X in the shortcut column, enabled only when there is a take).
  `POST /test/local-now` is the action; `evals/plan/cases_localnow.py` TN1–TN3.

## Custom gestures: any trigger, the same chord (2026-11-22)

Victor: *"un meniu 'custom' în gestures … echivalente pentru tastele care mie acum îmi vin din logi gestures.
cu un picker de key shortcuts care să permită de ex. cmd wheel click, sau opt-cmd-left click … mouse with 3
buttons + special keys pressed"*, and *"eu nu voi folosi vreodată, dar vreau să ușurez viața altora care nu au
mouse Logi, fără a mi-o complica pe a mea"*.

- **A trigger is translated into its Logi chord at the tap's entrance, nothing else** (`CustomGestures.swift`;
  `HotkeyTap.customGestureTakes` → `dispatchChord`, the trackpad's door, so it works in Logi and in Wheel
  mode). No branch is copied and none was changed: the one concession is `leftIsHeld || customForcesBind` in
  `case VK_F7`, because *Bind and prompt* is ⌃⌥⌘F7 with the left button down and no trigger can hold it.
  **Never give a custom action a branch of its own.**
- **Off by default, and off the tap asks one thing** — `customGestures.live` (ticked, or a release still owed),
  right after `wheelPressSeen` and before the frozen check. Nothing in the hint bar, the guide or the Logi path
  knows about it.
- **A trigger** = a mouse button (left, right, wheel pressed — never the side buttons) with ≥ 1 of ⌃⌥⇧⌘, or a
  key with ≥ 1 of ⌃⌥⌘; modifiers match exactly. Shown `⌘ wheel click`, `⌥⌘ click`, `⌃⇧ right click`, `⌃⌥K`;
  kept as `mouse:2:8` / `key:40:3:K`. Refused: a bare click, a bare (or ⇧-only) key, the trigger of another
  row, and the app's own keys (⌘⌃B, ⌘⌃X, ⌃P, ⌃⌥⌘F3…F12).
- **The release matches the press** (`area-crop.md`): the press is swallowed and remembered (`held`), its
  release is swallowed whatever is held by then, and any press that passes forgets the button — so neither a
  passed press with a swallowed release nor the reverse. A key's autorepeat is swallowed with it. **Nothing is
  posted**, so no modifier is left standing in the session (the stale-⌘ rule has nothing to guard here); his
  own fingers are still on the modifiers when the branch runs, exactly as under the trackpad's left ⌃⌥⌘.
- **Not a trigger:** this app's own events (`trackpadStamp`, `backButtonStamp`, `replayStamp`), anything while
  main is frozen, while a field in the window records, under the trackpad chord, and while a crop is up
  (`areaCropping` / `areaPressHeld` / `areaAwaitingDestination` — ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ mean something inside the selection).
  **The bare wheel is never one**, so the wheel-drag crop and the click that closes a tab are untouched.
- `CustomGestureAction.keyCode` is a third copy of the F-key numbers; `CustomGesturesTests` holds every row to
  `HotkeyTap.gestureNames` (glyph included) and requires a row for every chord there — **a chord added to the
  tap needs its row**. The tests feed `HotkeyTap.handle` itself (`takesForTesting`): ⌘ wheel click →
  `onPasteToggle` once, down and up taken, the bare wheel passing, *Bind and prompt* → `onGestureBindAndDictate`,
  a key trigger in both modes, a crop with a wheel trigger bound.
- **Not made by hand**: no real modifier-click has gone through the installed tap, and nothing was measured
  about a branch that posts keys (a paste, a Return) while his fingers still hold the trigger's modifiers.
