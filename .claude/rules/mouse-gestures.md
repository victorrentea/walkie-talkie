# Mouse gestures and the two global keys

What `HotkeyTap` may and may not do with the two keyboard chords and with the mouse, in both gesture modes (*Use Logi Gestures* ticked — the default — and unticked). Full history and reasoning: docs/journal.md — see the sections named after each rule below.

## The two global keys: ⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates

| key | call | note |
|---|---|---|
| **⌘⌃B** | `onBindHotkey` → `bindFrontmostTerminal` | was ⌘⌃D until 2026-09-01 |
| **⌘⌃D** | `onLocalToggle` → `toggleLocalRecording` | new — the wheel's click, from the keyboard |

- **Swallow both unconditionally, autorepeat included.** A held ⌘⌃D would open the microphone and shut it again on the next repeat; a held ⌘⌃B would bind and then immediately stop the session it started. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **⌘⌃D is ungated at the tap.** A key that sometimes falls through to macOS's "look up in dictionary" would be worse than one that never does; with nothing bound the sentence is held for five minutes and delivered when a bind lands (`awaitingBind`, 2026-09-11), so the press costs nothing. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **⌘⌃⌥D is Victor Addons' dark-mode toggle**, told apart by ⌥ alone. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*
- **This app is the only owner of ⌘⌃B/⌘⌃D.** Two taps claiming one chord would both fire on one press; nothing about either key remains in Victor Addons (the cheat sheet lists it display-only). Both shadow the system-wide chord. → journal: *⌘⌃D is this app's own key (since 2026-08-26)*
- **⌘⌃B on the target already bound unbinds** (`bindFrontmostTerminal(toggle:)` defaults to the toggle); only the mouse chord passes `false`. → journal: *The wheel is the relay's; mouse 5 is nobody's (except in Replace Wispr)*
- **Both ride the menu as real key equivalents** (`StatusItem`): ⌘⌃B on **Connect Window**, ⌘⌃D on **Start Dictation** *and* **End Dictation** — only one of the two rows is ever enabled. → journal: *⌘⌃B binds, ⌘⌃D dictates (since 2026-09-01)*

## Logi mode (default, ticked): the side buttons speak in function keys

In this mode the app takes **no mouse button at all**. Every mouse event is handed straight back one comparison later in `HotkeyTap.handle`; the side buttons arrive as ⌃⌥⌘F-key chords posted by Logi Options+.

| gesture | chord | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🔼 → | ⌃⌥⌘F10 | start the dictation, or end the one open (the same call ⌘⌃D makes) |
| 🔼 ← | ⌃⌥⌘F11 | cancel it — throw the audio away |
| 🔼 ↑ | ⌃⌥⌘F8 | dictate at a session that does not exist yet |
| ◀️ held, then 🔼 | ⌃⌥⌘F7 | **bind** the terminal in front — no toggle |
| 🔼 | ⌃⌥⌘F7 | a **prompt at the caret** — in both engines, whatever is bound (2026-09-12): the context frame, `[Dictated in RO or EN]`, highlights, picks, pictures — the terminal's envelope — and **submitted** when the caret is a Claude Code prompt (2026-09-23) |
| 🔽 ↓ | ⌃⌥⌘F12 | unbind — the menu's Disconnect |
| 🔽 | ⌃⌥⌘F6 | **a plain dictation** (Wispr Flow's hands-free toggle): start, or stop any Wispr sentence (2026-09-23) — **the words alone, at the caret even when bound**; no picture at the start or the end (⌃⌥P refused too), no probe, no kamikaze; a picture only while the relay's **own** sentence (forward button) records; never a Return |
| 🔽 → | ⌃⌥⌘F5 | **Return** (2026-09-23) — and **during a plain dictation: stop it, insert the words, then Return** (same day, later). Re-fires inside 0.6 s dropped, like F10 |
| 🔼 ↓ | ⌃⌥⌘F9 | **kamikaze** (2026-09-23): while a sentence is open or settling, appends `kamikaze` on its own line to the delivered prompt — the agent's cue to close its terminal when done. Again = take it back. While on, the chip carries a `☠️ Kamikaze` row under the others (`RelayWindow.setKamikaze`, shot `listening-kamikaze`); no flash either way — the row is the receipt. Not in the corpus copy; cleared on cancel and at the next sentence |
| 🔽 ↑ · 🔽 ← | ⌃⌥⌘F4 · F3 | assigned in Options+, unclaimed here — free rows |

- **Forward is a prompt, back is plain words** (2026-09-23, evening — `evals/test_gesture_spec.py` is the table, checked against the source; run it after touching any gesture). Victor: *"The forward click should be transcribing, basically prompting, at the carrot. There will be those metadata, the screenshot of the screen, the fact that is dictated … Clicking the back button on the mouse starts a plain voice transcription with no sorts of prompting tweaks around it. It should be just clean voice that I had."* Then: *"Enter is dispatched if I do a back click and move the mouse to my right"* and *"if I am putting my [caret] into a Claude Code terminal prompt, it should already submit the prompt."* `AppDelegate.caretPrompt` (set by `startDictation(paste:)`) makes the caret sentence `terminalLine`; `cleanSentence` (latched by `noteCleanStart` off `backStopsWispr`) makes it the words alone, at the caret even when bound — a bound delivery always presses Return, and here the Return is 🔽 →'s (`onBackSubmit` → `submitAfterCleanWords`). The Claude Code check is `TerminalBinding.frontClaudePromptTTY`: Terminal.app in front, a non-shell in front on the tab, a live `~/.claude/cwd/.last-<pid>` on the tty; IDE terminals are not covered. A sentence started with Wispr's own keyboard chord still follows the binding. → journal: *Forward is a prompt, back is plain words (2026-09-23)*
- **The back click follows the Engine** (2026-09-25). Victor: *"changing the transcription engine from wispr to elevenlabs should change it as well for «clean dictation» = back button click."* With the Engine off Wispr (`HotkeyTap.backUsesOwnEngine`, set in `wireDictationSource`) F6 no longer posts Wispr's chord: it calls `onCleanToggle` → `startDictation(paste: true, clean: true)`, the relay's own source with the same clean envelope, and a second click (or 🔽 →, which also queues the Return) stops it. `ownCleanSentence` tells that sentence apart from the forward click's, so the click is its stop and never its shutter; `cleanSentenceOpen` is what the context shot and ⌃⌥P refuse on. A Wispr sentence already open is still stopped with Wispr's chord, whatever the Engine.
- **Held right ⌘⌥ is the clean dictation too, on an Engine that is not Wispr** (2026-09-25). Victor: *"If I hold down the right command and the right option, that should enable the clean dictation for as long as I hold the two buttons."* `HotkeyTap.onCleanHold`: the press starts `startDictation(paste: true, clean: true)` (never over another sentence), the release ends it; a real key under the pair (`.shortcut`) or a hold under 0.35 s (`cleanHoldFloor`) cancels it quietly. The modifiers are watched, never swallowed. Wispr, if running, hears `54+61` too: `heldPairIsTheEngines` (held + 30 s) keeps `WisprFlowSource` from opening a sentence on its mic edge or rescuing its dropped ⌘V from the row, so the words land once. Engine = Wispr: unchanged, Wispr's own push-to-talk.
- **The back button's click and 🔽 → swapped roles on 2026-09-23** — this supersedes the three bullets below about the back click being *the stop of a 🔽 → dictation* and *Return at every other moment*. Victor: *"când apăs butonul de back, asta doar să oprească și să pornească dictarea curată, cum ar fi Wispr normal, iar enterul să-l trimit cu gestul de back și swipe la dreapta. Așa nu avem niciun fel de race pe cine ce face."* One click used to mean a stop, a picture or a Return depending on state he could not see, and a stop clicked a beat late typed a Return. The toggle reads *stop* from the arm first (`backStopsWispr`, true before Wispr's microphone warms up) and then the microphone; a second click inside `backToggleSettleSeconds` (0.8 s) is dropped, because the microphone reads stale for up to 0.6 s after a stop and would re-open it. **The stop is checked before the shutter** (same afternoon): the relay routes a clean sentence and goes `listening`, so `dictating` was true under it and the stop click became a picture (`📸 attached to in-flight dictation` twice at 14:24:57/14:25:00). `captureContext` skips the context shot while `backStopsWispr` — *"fără poze, fără nimic"*.
- **🔼 ↑ mid-sentence converts instead of doing nothing** (2026-09-22). Victor: *"atunci când, în timpul unei dictări, mă hotărăsc să pornesc într-un terminal nou, să meargă în continuare gestul de deschidere dictare în sus cu butonul de forward și în sus."* `onGestureSpawn` used to be guarded `!listening, !recordWhenSourceReady` and dropped the gesture on the floor mid-sentence — so the one moment the decision is actually made (half a sentence in, realising this belongs in a *new* terminal) was the one moment the button did nothing at all. It now calls `AppDelegate.convertDictationToSpawn()`, which is the wheel's own second-click conversion (`onWheelDoubleSpawn`) lifted into a shared method so the two gestures cannot drift: same recording, same words, only the destination changes, and the folder menu is offered exactly as at a fresh spawn. `recordWhenSourceReady` counts as a dictation there — on a cold engine the start banked the gesture and is waiting, and the resumed start reads `spawnPending`. Still a no-op for a sentence that is *already* a spawn or headed for the caret.
- **The side buttons never reach any tap; do not look for a hold.** Measured 2026-09-09: a probe reading raw HID input reports *below every event tap*, beside a `.cghidEventTap` listener, saw the wheel perfectly (`WHEEL ▼ … ▲ ținut 5272 ms`) and the side buttons **not once**, held or clicked; killing `com.logi.cp-dev-mgr` changed nothing (the divert lives in the mouse, over Logitech's GATT service on Bluetooth LE — there is no Bolt receiver). What arrives is what Options+ *synthesises*: `TAP tastă ▼ code=124 (pid 75980)`, pid 75980 being `logioptionsplus_agent`. The earlier "~18 ms down-and-up pair" claim was wrong. → journal: *The side buttons speak in function keys (2026-09-09)*
- **The numbers are duplicated in two places and must not drift**: Options+'s own custom-gesture screen, and `HotkeyTap`'s `VK_F3…VK_F12`. Change one and the gesture goes to whatever app claims that chord instead — silently, since a chord nothing handles is a chord nothing complains about. → journal: *The side buttons speak in function keys (2026-09-09)*
- **F10 (🔼 →) can re-trigger with no autorepeat flag** (2026-09-16). Victor: *"fac gestul de forward-dreapta și cumva cred că-l fac de două ori, pentru că se și pornește și se și închide dictarea instant"* — one flick toggling the microphone open and shut in the same beat. Options+'s gesture engine sometimes re-fires the tap on the tail of one continued motion, and that second tap carries no autorepeat bit (only real key-repeat does), so the existing guard let it through and the toggle undid itself. `lastF10At` / `gestureRetriggerSeconds` drop a second F10 arriving that fast. ~~Scoped to F10 only~~ — every flick has it since 2026-09-26, next bullet but one.
- **The re-trigger window slides, and it is 0.6 s** (2026-09-18) — the 0.35 s above was measured against the last F10 *acted on*, so a train of re-fires at ~300 ms let every second one through (dropped, acted on, dropped, acted on), and it was too short for a flick made slowly: Options+ re-fires for as long as the motion lasts, not once per gesture. A dropped tap now stamps `lastF10At` too, so the guard lasts as long as the movement does. → journal: *One slow flick right is one gesture, and it cannot close what it just opened (2026-09-18)*
- **Every flick has the same 0.6 s sliding window** (2026-09-26, batch 4; TG14, TG15): 🔼 ← F11, 🔼 ↑ F8, 🔼 ↓ F9, 🔽 ← F3, 🔽 ↑ F4, 🔽 ↓ F12 through `refired` / `lastFlickAt` (per key, a dropped one stamps too), F10 and F5 keep their own. Measured before: a kamikaze flick re-fired 300 ms later took itself back; a film flick started and stopped a two-frame recording. The line is F10's: `🎯 🔼 ↓ ⌃⌥⌘F9 re-triggered 300ms after the last one — still the same motion, dropped`. Autorepeat is swallowed once, above the switch, for all ten.
- **Every chord this app takes goes through `swallow()`** (2026-09-26, TG1, TG28), never a bare `return nil`: the ten gestures (named by `gestureNames`, the one list the trace, the re-fire guard and the frozen-app drop share), ⌘⌃B, ⌘⌃D, ⌘⇧P, bare F7/F9, the panel's ⏎/⎋, the Wheel-mode shutter Return. With `POST /test/key-trace` on, each key-down is `⌨️trace ↓ key N … — SWALLOWED by <branch>`; the trace used to show only the key-up passing.
- **The prompt panel's ⏎ and ⎋ are only keys he pressed** (2026-09-26, TG11). A key-down stamped `backButtonStamp` — 🔽 →'s `postReturn`, a clean sentence's `submitAfterCleanWords` — is not the panel's and goes on to the front app (the *Rebind to…* rule of batch 2, asked in the tap because this panel never takes the keyboard). The autorepeats of the ⏎ / ⎋ that answered the panel are eaten until the key-up (`panelReturnHeld`, `panelEscapeHeld`) — they used to reach Claude Code as extra Returns, or a second Escape (its rewind); a repeat of a key already down when the panel appeared answers nothing.
- **Cancel while the prompt panel is held cancels the panel** (Victor, Q6, 2026-09-26: *"cancel (🔼←, ✕, menu) while the prompt panel is held: cancels the panel"*): 🔼 ←, 🔽 ← (the relay-cancel half) and the menu's Cancel Dictation go through `AppDelegate.cancelSentenceOrPanel` → the ✕'s path (`cancelHeldPrompt` → `releaseHeld(send: false)`), one line `✕ cancelled — N chars never left the overlay (<gesture> while the panel was held — Q6)`. A microphone open on a newer sentence outranks the panel. **🔼 ↓ on the held panel marks that prompt kamikaze** (TG16 — it is not committed yet), again = off; **🔼 ↑ on a caret sentence stays ignored** (Q7) with one line, `✨ 🔼 ↑ on a caret sentence — ignored (Q7: it stays at the caret)`.
- **A start and its stop queued behind a frozen main thread are neither acted on** (2026-09-26, TL21): every toggle the tap hands over (F10, ⌘⌃D, the wheel's) is a `ToggleTicket`; a start that waited over a second on main with its stop already queued is dropped with it — the recorder never opens — `⏸ a start and its stop were both queued behind a frozen main thread …` and the chip flashes `⏸ ignored — the Mac was frozen`.
- **While the main thread is frozen the tap fails open, except for its own chords** (2026-09-26, TG25): ⌃⌥⌘F3–F12, ⌘⌃B and ⌘⌃D are dropped (`🧊 🔼 → ⌃⌥⌘F10 dropped — the main thread is frozen …`), decided on the event alone, and not acted on later; handed through they typed escape sequences into the Claude prompt and opened the dictionary. ⌘⇧P and bare F7/F9 are other apps' chords and pass. The gate itself: `dictation-source.md`, *A frozen app swallows nothing*.
- **A sentence younger than `gestureStopDwellSeconds` (2 s) cannot be ended by 🔼 →** (2026-09-18). Victor: *"if I hold down the forward button and move the mouse to the right, if I keep moving it to the right, that gesture both starts and then immediately ends the transcription … or at least just put a two seconds minimum threshold between stopping after starting"*. Read off `openSentenceAge`, which is `ownDictation`'s clock — so it covers the speculative ring and the caret as well. **Only the stop is guarded**: starting, the bind chord, the mid-sentence redirect, ⌘⌃D and the ⬅️ cancel are all untouched — throwing a sentence away must never be the gesture that waits. Price: a deliberate one-word dictation cannot be closed with the same flick for two seconds; ⌘⌃D closes it at once. → journal: *One slow flick right is one gesture, and it cannot close what it just opened (2026-09-18)*
- **The forward click (F7) is a caret dictation, in both engines, whatever is bound** (2026-09-12) — `onPasteToggle` → `startDictation(paste: true)`, and the source does the starting (Wispr by posting its own chord). It used to post the raw chord with Replace Wispr unticked, and that sentence was routed *by state* — to the bound terminal — which is the bug Victor found the evening the wrap shipped: *"apăsând butonul forward, click normal, el tot dictează legat de fereastră"*. His vocabulary: **🔼 click = caret (indiferent dacă e legat ceva); 🔼 → = legată; 🔼 ← = cancel (la caret sau legată); 🔼 ↑ = terminal nou — toate indiferent că Wispr sau modelul local e selectat.** The five-second bind grace that sent the click to the terminal is gone from this path for the same reason. → journal: *The forward click is the caret, and the arrow is the terminal (2026-09-12)*
- **⌃Escape typed by Victor is watched** (`onWisprMaybeCancelling`, 2026-09-12): it is Wispr's `dismiss` (`53+59`), and a sentence dismissed on Wispr's side pastes nothing, so the source ends the capture and the ring at once instead of at `settleTimeout`. Never taken; this app's own `postWisprCancel` is stamped and not reported. → journal: *The ring goes down when he dismisses in Wispr (2026-09-12)*
- **Historical — the forward click used to post Wispr's chord.** `postWisprHandsFree` types `fn ⌃ Space` — `fn ⌃ Space`, read from `prefs.user.shortcuts` in `~/Library/Application Support/Wispr Flow/config.json` as `"49+59+63": "popo"` (also there: `"54+61": "ptt"`, `"178+59+63": "lens"`, `"53+59": "dismiss"`). - **Press the chord's modifiers as keys, not just flags, and wait `settleForOptionsPlus` first.** Wispr stores three keycodes (49 Space, 59 Control, 63 fn), so fn and Control go out as real `flagsChanged` events around the Space — four events, correct under either reading — after the same settle `postReturn` waits, because it is posted from the F7 callback while Options+ has ⌃⌥⌘ on the wire. → journal: *The forward click starts Wispr Flow too, because Wispr cannot take the button (2026-09-09)*
- **The bind chord is the left button held, then the forward click — sharing F7.** Judged like the old left-plus-wheel chord: `leftIsHeld` = genuinely down (the window server is asked as well as our own bookkeeping) for `chordHoldSeconds` (0.3 s). A *click* leaves nothing held (`leftDownAt` cleared at release), so click-into-a-field-then-forward-click still dictates at the caret. **Not gated on Replace Wispr** — it says *where the words go*. **F9 is a free row**, not a second way in. → journal: *The bind is a chord again: hold the left button, click the forward one (2026-09-09)*
- **`postReturn` must sleep past Options+'s trailing flags-cleared event.** Posted inside the F6 callback the Return reached the front app as `code=36 mods=CTRL+OPT+CMD`; Terminal hands that to Claude Code as ⌥⏎ and the button inserted blank lines instead of sending the prompt. Two fixes measured and rejected: `down.flags = []` (clean against a synthesised chord, dirty against the real button — the window server merges live state back into a posted key), and polling `CGEventSource.flagsState` (answers *clean* through the whole window, because Options+ never presses ⌃⌥⌘ as keys; the Return went out 2 ms after the F6). The gap F6 → flags-cleared measured 12, 15 and 22 ms; `settleForOptionsPlus` is 45 ms, off the tap thread, with the modifier poll kept *after* it. Any key this app posts near a gesture has the same trap waiting (the selection watcher's ⌘C was caught as `code=8 mods=CTRL+OPT+CMD`). → journal: *The bind is a chord again: hold the left button, click the forward one (2026-09-09)*
- **The back click is the stop of the dictation 🔽 → started** (2026-09-17). Victor: *"butonul de Back trebuie să se transforme în a opri Wispr Flow din dictare. Să nu mai fie necesar să fac, încă o dată, gestul de Back cu dreapta … și după ce Wispr Flow nu mai dictează, revine butonul de back la tasta obișnuită de Enter."* The flick that starts it is made with the back button held, so the thumb is already on the one button that can end it; F5 arms `wisprGestureAt` and F6 consumes it, posting **the same `postWisprHandsFree` chord the second flick would have**, so the two stops cannot drift apart.
  - **The arm is decided on Wispr's own microphone, not on the relay's `listening`.** 🔽 → posts Wispr's chord raw, so with the **Engine on the local model** nothing in this app is watching Wispr at all — and that is exactly the configuration he dictates into other applications from. `AppDelegate.wisprMic` is a second `WisprWatch` with **no `onChange`**, sampled from the tap thread (`sampleIsRunningInput`, three CoreAudio reads over a cached id list). `wisprSource`'s own watch could not be borrowed: its `onChange` drives `edge(_:measured:)`, which opens captures and meters on a source that may not be the one wired up.
  - **The arm is retired by a watch, not only by being read.** A reading is not an ending: a 🔽 → dictation that stops on its own — Wispr's silence timeout, a ⌃Escape, the window closed — would leave `wisprGestureAt` standing, and the next time Wispr's microphone opened for some *other* reason the back button would read that as its own sentence still running. `AppDelegate.watchBackStop` polls the sampler every 250 ms **for the length of the arm and no longer**, and `HotkeyTap.retireWisprStop` takes it down on the first close after an open (or when the grace expires with no open at all). A **poll and not `onChange`**: the CoreAudio notification is 0–6 s late and produced no edge at all in five of five successful runs (2026-09-13).
  - **A cold-start grace of 12 s covers the gap before the microphone opens** — 324–674 ms warm, 5–6 s cold (2026-09-12), the measurement `speculativeGrace` is built on. Once the microphone closes the button is Return again with no edge to be told about, which is what makes the arm impossible to leave standing.
  - **The claim is consumed at the click, not at the microphone's close.** That close is a poll away, and a second back click inside it would post the toggle again and **open** a dictation — worse than a missing Return.
  - **The arm survives a restart** (2026-09-17, evening) — `Relaunch.stashBackStop` parks it on disk beside the binding whenever it *moves*, not at the restart, because `relay-restart.sh` kills from outside, `build-app.sh` replaces the bundle and a crash announces nothing. `AppDelegate.restoreBackStopAfterRestart` puts it back on **two** conditions: a marker younger than three minutes **and** Wispr's microphone open right now, asked 1.2 s after launch because `WisprWatch` answers *closed* until it has enumerated the audio process list. The age travels with the marker so the 12 s cold-start grace is not handed out twice. This is how the feature was lost the day it shipped: a 🔽 → dictation at 19:29:06 and a relaunch eight seconds into it, after which the click typed a Return into his terminal.
  - **…and since 2026-09-18 it stops ANY Wispr dictation, not only the one 🔽 → started.** Victor: *"back button alone still doesn't stop ongoing wisprflow dictation."* Where the previous bullet **logged** a Return typed over an open microphone and left it at that, the click now posts `postWisprHandsFree` and swallows the Return. The arm was never the point — it was the evidence; what he wants on this button while any Wispr sentence runs is the way out of it, and the same morning he asked for the ⚡ ring to cover the same set (`AppDelegate.wisprHearing`). A beacon that says *something is listening* over a button that will not stop it is half a gesture. `⌨️ ⬅️ back button — Return, though Wispr Flow's microphone is open: …` therefore no longer happens; the line is `🎙️ ⬅️ back button — stopping Wispr Flow's dictation (not one 🔽 → started; no Return typed)`.
    - **Gated on `ownDictation`, not on `dictating`.** While the relay is listening this button is the **shutter**, and `dictating` is only `hasDestination && listening` — an unbound or speculative relay dictation falls through to this branch with Wispr's microphone open, because it is the relay that opened it, and would be stopped by a click meant to type a Return. `ownDictation` (`listening || source.isRecording || speculative || settling`) is false for exactly the foreign sentences this is about.
    - **Both stops tell the state machine now** (`onWisprRawChord`), the armed one as well as this one. The chord this app posts is filtered out of the tap's own keyboard branch, so without it the machine never learns the sentence is over — and 🔽 → never learned it had begun. → `.claude/rules/dictation-source.md`
    - **The cost, accepted:** a back click made to type a Return while Wispr happens to be listening types nothing. That is the same trade the shutter makes for the length of a relay dictation, and the log names it.
  - **It outranks the shutter, and only for this one kind of sentence.** A 🔽 → sentence is Wispr's own: the relay rings for it and routes nothing, so a picture taken during it has no message to attach to. Every relay-started dictation keeps the shutter on this button.
- **The back button's Return is posted by this app in Logi mode.** Options+ owns the button, nothing upstream types anything: `HotkeyTap.postReturn` makes it, stamped `backButtonStamp` so the tap can tell it from a Return Victor typed. **If that branch is ever removed, the key he submits with all day stops existing.** → journal: *Use Logi Gestures — the tick that chooses between the two sets*
- **The default is on, and it has to be written out.** `UserDefaults.bool(forKey:)` answers `false` for a key never written, which would ship the wheel gestures to the Mac already configured for the new ones. The row — `Mouse Gestures: Logi` / `: Wheel`, a submenu since 2026-09-14 — reads `object(forKey:) as? Bool ?? true`. → journal: *Use Logi Gestures — the tick that chooses between the two sets*
- **The tap subscribes to the mouse in both modes.** The event mask is fixed when the tap is created and the tick can flip at any moment; rebuilding the tap on a toggle would tear down the thing carrying ⌘⌃B while a dictation may be running. → journal: *Use Logi Gestures — the tick that chooses between the two sets*
- **The menu legend column carries both legends and picks one.** `restyleGestures` runs on the toggle as well as on every open, and the tab stop is measured against the wider of the two sets so the column cannot resize under the pointer. → journal: *Use Logi Gestures — the tick that chooses between the two sets*
- **Keep the wheel set compiled in, not commented out.** The Logi gestures live in an Options+ profile on *this* Mac; a fresh install or a training-room machine has no side buttons until that profile is rebuilt, and the wheel path needs nothing but the app. → journal: *Use Logi Gestures — the tick that chooses between the two sets*

## Wheel mode (unticked): still live code

Off by default since 2026-09-09. Everything in this section is what the menu's *Use Logi Gestures* turns back on when unticked; with it ticked the wheel is untouched and the side buttons arrive as chords instead. Middle-click-to-close-a-tab is what the default mode exists to protect (*"ideea e sa evit sa emit middle click, caci folosesc middle click sa inchid de ex taburi chrome/vsc"*).

| state | press | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| bound, not dictating | **click** | start a dictation |
| dictating | **click** | end it — transcribe and send |
| dictating | hold **2s** | **cancel** it — throw the audio away |
| **any state, bound or not** | **⌘ + click** | start a dictation **at a session that does not exist yet** — see *⌘ + the wheel* |
| anywhere, any state | **left button held ≥0.3s, then click the wheel** | **bind** — same call as ⌘⌃B, **without the toggle** |
| anywhere, any state | **left button held ≥0.3s, then the wheel held 1s** | bind **and** start the dictation at it |
| bound, any state | **right button down, then click the wheel** | **disconnect** — same call as the menu's Disconnect |
| anywhere, any state | **right button down, then the wheel held 1s** | dictate at a terminal that does not exist yet — ⌘ + the wheel, without the keyboard |
| bound, not dictating | **click twice** | the dictation the first click started **becomes** a spawn — new session, folder menu on the second click |
| nothing bound, no chord | click | passed straight through |

- **The wheel is the relay's whenever the relay runs — bound or not.** `localCapture` is true whether or not anything is bound since 2026-09-11 (`AppDelegate.syncLocalCapture`), so the table's last row and the unbound double-click branch (`idleWheelClickAt`, first click passed through, second swallowed with `wheelArmed` set / `wheelDown` cleared, carrying `wheelHeldFromPress` for the context shot) are unreachable through it. They are left standing because they are what has to work again if `holdsForBind` is ever flipped back. → journal: *Double-clicking the wheel turns the dictation into a spawn (2026-09-05)*
- **Judge the double click before `dictating` is consulted.** The test is `wheelDictateAt`, a stamp only *the bare wheel opening a dictation* sets — no chord and no cancel can be doubled into a spawn. Consulting `dictating` first is what killed the 2 s spawn hold: the flag only arrives from `syncBorrowedGestures` once the recording is up, ten seconds late on a cold model. → journal: *Double-clicking the wheel turns the dictation into a spawn (2026-09-05)*
- **A cold model converts like a warm one** (2026-09-26): nothing is banked any more — the first click opens the microphone at once (the WAV waits for the weights at the stop), so the second click finds `listening` and converts. `recordWhenModelReady`/`recordWhenSourceReady` and the `resumed:` start are gone. → journal: *Fixes to the test plan's findings, batch 3*
- **The second press is swallowed and left unclaimable.** It sets `wheelArmed` (the app underneath never sees a middle-up without its middle-down) and clears `wheelDown`, so `tapped` is false at the release — otherwise the click that re-aimed the dictation would immediately end it. → journal: *Double-clicking the wheel turns the dictation into a spawn (2026-09-05)*
- **The bare wheel starts without `captureContext` (`deferContext`)**; the first release takes the shot, so the picture is of the screen his finger left. The audio's zero stays the press. Every other route in (⌘⌃D, the menu, the chords) captures at the press. This is the only place `deferContext` is reachable. → journal: *Double-clicking the wheel turns the dictation into a spawn (2026-09-05)*
- **The chord does not toggle (2026-09-01).** `bindFrontmostTerminal(toggle:)` is the switch; only the chord passes `false`. Letting go already has two routes that mean nothing else — the right-held chord and the menu's Disconnect; a toggle is a trap on a gesture that has them. → journal: *The wheel is the relay's; mouse 5 is nobody's (except in Replace Wispr)*
- **Hold the wheel a further second after the left chord (`chordDictateSeconds`) and the microphone opens at the terminal just bound.** The bind still fires at the press — nothing about it is conditional on the hold. → journal: *Double-clicking the wheel turns the dictation into a spawn (2026-09-05)*
- **The right chord is judged at the press, with no hold to wait out.** The `chordHoldSeconds` threshold on it was a bug: right-press then wheel land inside 0.3 s often enough that the press fell through to the bare-wheel branch and opened a dictation *at the terminal already bound* (*"if I repeat it a bunch of times, I make it work eventually"*, 2026-09-04). `wheelRightChord` went with the second reading (2026-09-06); there is nothing left for the release to disambiguate. → journal: *The right chord has no hold to wait out (2026-09-04)*
- **`rightIsHeld` asks the window server too** (`rightDownAt > 0 || CGEventSource.buttonState(.combinedSessionState, button: .right)`): a press the tap never saw — re-enabled after a timeout, or a context menu's tracking loop in the way — leaves `rightDownAt` at zero with the finger down. **`reconcileButtons()` runs before any chord is judged** for the reverse staleness: a release the tap never saw would leave a button held for good and read every bare wheel click as a chord. → journal: *The right chord has no hold to wait out (2026-09-04)*
- **Disconnect is the first middle-button branch in `handle`.** It outranks a held prompt and a running dictation — the answer to *stop, this is going to the wrong place* — and a press it takes mid-dictation cancels the pending 2 s hold timer. The press is swallowed either way (`wheelArmed` claims the release). → journal: *The right chord means one thing again (2026-09-06)*
- **Gate it on `HotkeyTap.bound`, deliberately not `localCapture`.** The flag that means *there is a binding to let go of* must not be the one that means *the wheel may open the microphone*. With nothing bound the branch is skipped and the click stays available to whatever is underneath. → journal: *The right chord has no hold to wait out (2026-09-04)*
- **The left and right buttons are watched and never taken.** `.leftMouseDown`/`.leftMouseUp` are on the tap only to record *when* the button went down, returned untouched at the top of `handle`; a swallowed right click is a context menu that never opened. Nothing in that file may ever swallow one — it is how the Mac is used. → journal: *The right chord has no hold to wait out (2026-09-04)*
- **Do not put the 1 s start hold back.** The price of this mode is that while the relay runs middle-click in a browser does not open or close tabs; if it grates, the cheap fix is to hand the click back when the frontmost app is a browser, not the hold. → journal: *The wheel is the relay's; mouse 5 is nobody's (except in Replace Wispr)*

## Rules that hold in both modes

- **Wheel held and turned over the ring is the halo dial** (2026-09-20, `HotkeyTap.haloDial`): the next halo on a notch up, the previous on a notch down, round the list and back to the lightning film — the same list the menu's `Halo fx` row shows. **It writes the preference of the destination being worn** (2026-09-21, `dialStyle` → `haloStyle.<caret|bound|spawn>`), which it can because it turns only while the ring is up; the chip flashes the name and the destination (`✨ Tendrils · Bound terminal`). F7/F9 step the same list but only draw. **Gated on `haloUp`**, pushed from `syncBorrowedGestures` with the ring's own expression (`listening || speculative || wisprHearing`); with the ring down, or with the middle button up, **every scroll on the machine passes through untouched** — `.scrollWheel` joined the tap mask for this one branch and nothing else reads it. The button is asked of `CGEventSource.buttonState` (both state ids), because in Logi mode the press leaves no mark in this tap. **Same button as the crop drag, told apart by what the hand does**: the crop arms on travel past `areaDragThreshold`, the dial on the wheel turning; the first to happen owns the press — `haloDialed` refuses the crop for the rest of a press that has dialed, `areaCropping` refuses the dial (its scrolls pass through). In Wheel mode the dial claims the press as the drag does (`claimWheelPress`, hold cancelled), so the release ends nothing. One step per 120 ms, so a spin cannot skip three halos. Not fakeable from `.build/debug` (no tap); `WT_HALO_CYCLE=<s>` with `WT_HALO_DEMO` turns the dial on the live ring instead. → `.claude/rules/replace-wispr-and-halo.md`, *Other halos*
- **Mouse 4 is the shutter only while `listening`.** `AppDelegate.syncBorrowedGestures()` pushes it to the tap from both edges (a dictation starting, ending), and sets **both** borrowed gestures — this button and ⌘⇧-click in Chrome — from one expression, so the recording row can never advertise one in a state where it is dead. Borrowed in Replace Wispr too since 2026-09-08 (`live`, full stop). At rest the button is Return: `postReturn` in Logi mode, Victor Addons' `BackButtonEnter` with the tick off. → journal: *Mouse 4 is the shutter, but only while dictating*
- **Only the bare press, and both halves swallowed** (`otherMouseDown` *and* `otherMouseUp` — why the tap mask grew): ⌘+button is a separate mapping downstream, so any modifier passes straight through, and a downstream tap would otherwise see an orphan release to act on. → journal: *Mouse 4 is the shutter, but only while dictating*
- **Detect the Return-in-disguise by `backButtonStamp`, never by process name.** `0x7774_4241_434B_0000` in `eventSourceUserData`, set by Addons' `BackButtonEnter.post` / this app's `postReturn`, duplicated across the two repos and must not drift. On 2026-09-08 Addons was the newer tap (relay up since 22:14, addons since 22:37) and every press became a Return before this tap saw a button — the shot silently stopped and the Enter scrolled the Chrome page; Addons also posts genuine Returns (`KeySimulator`), so a name cannot tell them apart. Restart order is now irrelevant: whichever tap is first, the shot is taken exactly once. → journal: *Mouse 4 is the shutter, but only while dictating*
- **The shutter and the ⌘⇧ pick cannot collide, and the ⌘C probe cannot poison an armed pick.** `inspect.js` treats any keydown during a ⌘⇧ hold as proof the chord is a shortcut and abandons the arm — but both shutter routes require `bare`, so with ⌘⇧ held it never fires. Do not relax that gate without re-reading this. → journal: *The shutter and the ⌘⇧ pick are two gestures, and they cannot collide*
- **A press with a hold on it is claimed, not tested.** The timer (main queue) and the release (tap thread) both read `wheelDown` and acted, so a button let go in the millisecond the timer fired ran **both** halves — a disconnect *and* a spawn, or `mic.start` twice. `HotkeyTap.claimWheelPress()` takes it under `stateLock`; it sets `wheelArmed` **before** clearing `wheelDown`, because the release's swallow test reads `wheelArmed || wheelDown` and a window where neither is true is an orphan middle-up. `AppDelegate.startLocalRecording` refuses re-entry as the second net. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **Nothing is replayed.** `replayMiddleClick` / `wheelReplayUntil` are gone. One rule at the release: **any release whose press we swallowed is ours**, whatever the state has become — the left button may have come up, the binding dropped; the app underneath must never be handed a middle-up it never saw a middle-down for. That is the orphan-event bug this file guards against, written a third time. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **Cancelling costs a 2 s hold, and the state at the press and at the fire must agree.** The state at the press picks the timer; a dictation that ended under his finger cannot have its cancel land on the next one. Same verdict as the menu's `Cancel Dictation`. The flash says `Cancelled`, not `Dictation aborted`. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **Cancelled audio is kept five minutes** (*"reține înregistrarea audio respectivă pe disk după cancel 5 minute … + menu entry de rigoare"*): in `~/Library/Caches/…/cancelled/`, a *sibling* of `shots` (`ScreenCapture.prune` walks that folder counting `.jpg`s); deleted by a timer in the running process, and a launch empties the folder outright. One is kept — a second cancel replaces the first. Words only: frames, picks and highlights stay cleared. Destination decided at recovery (bound → the terminal, otherwise the caret), not at the cancel. A menu row (`Recover Cancelled Dictation`) and deliberately not a gesture; `POST /test/recover` from a desk. The recovered audio is filed in the corpus. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **A toggle, not a push-to-talk.** A dictation aimed at an agent runs to a minute or more, and a mouse button held for a minute is a hand that cannot take the screenshots the same minute exists for. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **`frontIsBindable` is still pushed from `AppDelegate` on every app activation** — the wheel no longer consults it (the chord acts wherever it is made and lets `bindFrontmostTerminal` refuse), but the menu's **Connect Terminal** row greys itself out with it. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **The menu can start, end and cancel a dictation.** `Start Dictation` / `End Dictation` / `Cancel Dictation` call the same `stopLocalRecording()` a wheel tap does; the rows are always visible and merely disabled, since they are the only lines that say whether the microphone is open, and read `StatusItem.isRecording` on open. The wheel is one button on one specific mouse whose battery goes. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*
- **`MicRecorder` records 16 kHz mono 16-bit through `AVAudioConverter`** from the device's native rate; anything under 0.35 s is dropped as a misfire; the microphone is asked for **while the model loads**, not at the first press — the grant dialog is modal and mid-sentence with an agent waiting is the wrong moment. A recording is sent even below the confidence floor; the banner says the score. → journal: *A cancelled sentence is kept for five minutes (2026-09-10)*

## Do not

- **The numbers are duplicated in two places and must not drift**: Options+'s own custom-gesture screen, and `HotkeyTap`'s `VK_F3…VK_F12`.
- **The literal is duplicated across the two repos and must not drift** (`backButtonStamp`, `0x7774_4241_434B_0000`).
- **If that branch is ever removed, the key he submits with all day stops existing** (`HotkeyTap.postReturn`).
- **Do not relax that gate without re-reading this** (`bare` on both shutter routes).
- **Nothing in that file may ever swallow one** (a left or right button event).
- **`holdsForBind` must never become a menu tick**; Pause is gone (2026-09-01).
- **The halo and the chip are veiled for the length of a wheel-drag crop** (2026-09-23; the chip the same day, `RelayWindow.veiled` hides `root`) — `CaretHalo.veiled` hides the ring's and the heads' content views (not `alphaValue`, which `refresh` rewrites at 20 Hz); everything keeps running, and it is back at the release or the cancel.
